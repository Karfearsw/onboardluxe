TCPA & Compliance Basics

SDR · Agent · VA 6 min read Knowledge Check (3 questions · pass 3/3)
Research, Not Legal Advice

This lesson summarizes public research for training purposes. It is not legal advice and counsel must confirm anything you rely on in practice. When in doubt, ask your manager before you dial.

Why the TCPA Matters to Us

The Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) is the federal law governing calls and texts to consumers. Violations carry $500–$1,500 per message or call in statutory damages — one bad campaign can bankrupt a company. Our entire outreach model is built around staying on the safe side of it:

  • We do manual voice calls. Our agents and VAs dial manually from the power dialer. We do not use autodialers, robocalls, or AI-voice callers without express consent.
  • We do not cold-text. Unsolicited marketing texts are the most-litigated channel in America. Ocean Luxe does not send cold texts — especially not to skip-traced numbers (see Lesson 08), where there is zero relationship and zero consent.
  • Lists are scrubbed before they reach you. National and state Do-Not-Call registries are applied at the list level. Scrubbing is a safety net, not a guarantee — a person telling you "don't call" always wins.

The Four Rules Every Agent Must Know

  1. Quiet hours: 8 AM – 9 PM, the lead's local time. Never dial outside this window for the number's area. If the lead is in Michigan and you're in Florida, it's their clock that matters. The CRM shows the lead's timezone — check it when the geography is unfamiliar.
  2. Honor every opt-out immediately. "Take me off your list," "don't call," "remove me" — set the DNC flag at once, stop all outreach, and never call them back without express re-consent. Opt-outs are company-wide: no teammate may contact them either.
  3. Never call a DNC-flagged lead. The flag survives pipeline moves, lead transfers, and re-imports. If the CRM says Do Not Contact, you don't dial. Period.
  4. Florida is stricter. Florida's mini-TCPA (state telemarketing law) has its own requirements beyond the federal rules — and FL is one of our core markets. Treat every FL lead with extra care: double-check DNC status, keep calls professional, and log everything.
State Watchlist

We operate in MA, RI, FL, and MI. Massachusetts has a quarterly state DNC registry in addition to the national one — FL and MA compliance is enforced at the list level before leads reach your queue, but the agent's job is to honor any opt-out or DNC complaint the moment it happens.

What NOT to Do (Ever)

  • Never text a skip-traced number. A found number is not consent. $500–$1,500 per message.
  • Never use pre-recorded or AI voices to call a consumer without their express prior consent.
  • Never misrepresent who you are. State your name, Ocean Luxe, and why you're calling — every time. (Also covered in Lesson 01.)
  • Never buy or use breached/hacked data for outreach. If a list looks shady, report it to your manager.
  • Never re-dial an opt-out "to be sure." One opt-out ends the conversation forever.
Key Principle

Compliance is a feature of our sales process, not a tax on it. Clean outreach keeps our numbers from being flagged, our lists callable, and the company out of court — which is how we stay in business long enough to get rich.
